# ❤️ Human Heart – 3D Digital Twin

### An Interactive 3D Educational Simulation of the Human Heart

## 📌 Project Overview

The Human Heart Digital Twin is an interactive
3D educational project designed to help medical
students understand cardiac anatomy, physiology,
biological mechanisms, and electrical activity.

The project integrates 3D modeling, visualization,
and physiological simulation to create an
interactive medical learning environment.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Visualize human heart anatomy in 3D.
- Understand cardiac physiological functions.
- Explore the cardiac electrical conduction system.
- Demonstrate the 12-lead ECG electrode placement.
- Simulate selected cardiac arrhythmias.
- Support medical and biomedical engineering education.

## 🫀 Main Features

### 1. Cardiac Anatomy
- External heart structures.
- Internal heart chambers.
- Heart valves and major vessels.

### 2. Cardiac Physiology
- Cardiac cycle.
- Blood circulation.
- Physiological mechanisms.

### 3. Electrical Activity
- Cardiac conduction system.
- Electrical impulses.
- 12-lead ECG electrode placement.

### 4. Cardiac Arrhythmia Simulation
- Ventricular Fibrillation (VF).
- Atrial Fibrillation (AF).
- Ventricular Tachycardia (VT).
- Bradycardia.
